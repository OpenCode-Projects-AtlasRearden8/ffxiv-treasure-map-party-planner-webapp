FFXIV Treasure Map Party Planner

Offline helper for map hunts — no account, everything stays in your browser.

Party & loot needs

Add everyone in the party and what each person is hunting: Gil (selling loot flat out), Kindred (keeping items, e.g. glamour, Orchestrion rolls, furnishings), or Either (flexible). The app splits needs so you can wave off pass rules cleanly.

Map log

Log maps as the party finds them. Nothing is scraped — you type what you found.

Roll ledger

Log each contested roll (e.g. /random results) for an item, then mark which roll tipped (won) the item. The ladder and “what tipped” history build automatically.

Loot-split ladder

Your fair-loot priority ladder, computed live from the roll ledger: fewest tipped wins first, alphabetical as a tiebreak. Top of the ladder goes first on the next contested drop.

Want a different ladder order? That is a house rule — add it on the House Rules tab so the party agrees before the next portal.

Ward zone picker

Tick who is around, set the number of ward zones to assign, and the app deals pick order in a clean serpentine rotation (first player, last player, back up), so nobody hoards the good zones and nobody gets skipped.

House rules

The fun extra: players type in their own party rules (the app honors them). Active rules are shown as reminders on the Rolls tab. Uncheck or remove rules the party voted down.

Data (local only)

Everything is stored in this browser only. Export your JSON before switching devices or browsers; import it back later. Nothing is ever sent anywhere.
